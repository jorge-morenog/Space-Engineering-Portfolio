# Assembly, Integration and Testing

This section presents selected projects related to spacecraft assembly, integration and mechanical testing, developed during the MSc in Space Systems at UPM.

The projects cover the preparation and execution of a CubeSat assembly procedure and the definition of qualification-level sinusoidal and random vibration tests for a spacecraft electronics box.

The work includes assembly planning, mechanical integration, cleanliness practices, hardware identification, test requirements, test configuration, instrumentation, mechanical ground support equipment, test sequencing, pass/fail criteria and correlation with finite element analysis.

## Projects

### [CubeSat 1U Assembly and Integration Procedure](https://github.com/jorge-morenog/Space-Engineering-Portfolio/blob/main/mechanical-design-ait/assembly-integration-testing/T1_AIT_Assembly.pdf)

Preparation and execution of a detailed assembly procedure for a CubeSat 1U educational model.

The procedure defines the applicable documentation, tools, cleaning materials, fastening elements, component lists and step-by-step integration tasks. It includes exploded CAD views, equipment orientation, assembly constraints, operator requirements, photographic evidence and verification notes obtained during the physical integration campaign.

Main activities include:

- Preparation of the assembly sequence
- Identification of components and fastening hardware
- Cleanliness and contamination-control practices
- Integration of the structural frame and internal equipment trays
- Installation of electronic equipment and mechanical supports
- Integration of the tray stack into the primary structure
- Installation of closing panels and solar-cell supports
- Identification of tolerances, accessibility constraints and assembly risks

### [Qualification Vibration Test Specification and Procedure](https://github.com/jorge-morenog/Space-Engineering-Portfolio/blob/main/mechanical-design-ait/assembly-integration-testing/T2_AIT_Testing_TSPE_%26_TPRO.pdf)

Definition of the test specification and test procedure for qualification-level sinusoidal and random vibration testing of a dummy spacecraft electronics box.

The project establishes the requirements to be verified, test levels, test configuration, mechanical and electrical ground support equipment, accelerometer locations, execution sequence and pass/fail criteria.

The accelerometer configuration was selected using the expected modal behaviour obtained from an MSC Patran/Nastran finite element model, linking numerical structural analysis with physical test preparation.

Main activities include:

- Definition and traceability of structural verification requirements
- Low-level sine, qualification sine and random-vibration test sequencing
- Definition of sinusoidal and PSD test environments
- Selection and positioning of control and response accelerometers
- Definition of shaker, fixture, MGSE and EGSE requirements
- Mechanical installation and instrumentation procedures
- Real-time test monitoring and emergency-stop considerations
- Comparison of pre-test and post-test natural frequencies
- Definition of pass/fail criteria and test documentation

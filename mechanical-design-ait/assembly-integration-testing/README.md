# Assembly, Integration and Testing

Selected projects in spacecraft assembly, integration and mechanical testing, developed during the MSc in Space Systems at UPM.

## Projects

### [CubeSat 1U Assembly and Integration Procedure](https://github.com/jorge-morenog/Space-Engineering-Portfolio/blob/main/mechanical-design-ait/assembly-integration-testing/T1_AIT_Assembly.pdf)

Preparation and execution of a detailed assembly procedure for a CubeSat 1U educational model.

- Assembly sequence and hardware identification
- Cleanliness and integration practices
- Mechanical integration of structure, trays and equipment
- Assembly constraints, tolerances and accessibility considerations

### [Qualification Vibration Test Specification and Procedure](https://github.com/jorge-morenog/Space-Engineering-Portfolio/blob/main/mechanical-design-ait/assembly-integration-testing/T2_AIT_Testing_TSPE_%26_TPRO.pdf)

Definition of qualification-level sinusoidal and random vibration tests for a spacecraft electronics box.

- Verification requirements and test sequencing
- Sinusoidal, random and low-level sine environments
- Accelerometer selection and positioning
- MGSE/EGSE definition, pass/fail criteria and FEM-to-test correlation
